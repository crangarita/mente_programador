## Brand & Style

The brand identity targets Gen Z high school students (aged 15–18) attending university recruitment fairs. The experience transforms prospective engineering discovery into a high-octane, interactive digital gauntlet: "¿Tienes mente de programador?". The tone is exhilarating, empowering, futuristic, and authentic to modern gaming culture without descending into juvenile tropes. It treats visitors like nascent digital architects stepping into a command terminal of the future.

The aesthetic fuses **Cyber-Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast Neon Precision**. Key attributes include:
- Deep, immersive midnight tones providing atmospheric canvas depth.
- Kinetic luminescent accents that deliver immediate visual feedback and game-like progression queues.
- Frosted micro-surfaces with razor-sharp refractive borders, simulating cutting-edge holographic heads-up displays (HUDs).
- Segmented archetype identities representing tech archetypes (Frontend, Backend, AI, Security, Data, Game Dev), celebrating individual affinity and technical style.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep dark mode, maximizing OLED contrast ratios and giving luminous elements optical vibrance.

### Archetype Profile System
Each vocational identity claims a dedicated spectrum value used dynamically in HUD cards, badges, and diagnostic outcomes:
- **AI Explorer (Primary System Hub):** Cyber Cyan (`#06B6D4`, Highlight: `#22D3EE`). Symbolizes algorithmic intelligence and neural networks.
- **Game Builder (Secondary Core):** Neon Violet (`#8B5CF6`, Highlight: `#A855F7`). Expresses interactive logic, 3D worlds, and creative compute.
- **Backend Architect (Tertiary Driver):** Electric Blue (`#2563EB`, Highlight: `#3B82F6`). Reflects infrastructure, robust scalability, and foundational engines.
- **Frontend Creator:** Neon Magenta (`#EC4899`, Highlight: `#F472B6`). Represents reactive design, visual craft, and sensory interfaces.
- **Cyber Guardian:** Emerald Matrix Green (`#10B981`, Highlight: `#34D399`). Denotes perimeter security, cryptography, and systems defense.
- **Data Detective:** Solar Amber (`#F59E0B`, Highlight: `#FBBF24`). Captures analytical power, predictive intelligence, and telemetry.

### Neutral & Background Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#0B0F19` (Obsidian Deep).
- **Surface Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#111827` at 70% opacity with 16px backdrop blur.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Raised Terminals/Modals):** `#1F2937` at 80% opacity.
- **Surface Borders:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` resting; dynamic profile color at `0.45` opacity on focus/hover.
- **Text Levels:** High-emphasis `#F9FAFB`, medium-emphasis `#9CA3AF`, muted/disabled `#4B5563`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances punchy display titles, rapid readability for gamified multiple-choice challenges, and technical credibility:

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers clean geometry, aerodynamic confidence, and aggressive modernism. Headings demand immediate visual dominance at physical booth screens and handheld touch tablets.
- **Body & Longform (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse that preserves clarity during fast-paced decision prompts and diagnostic profiles.
- **Code, Metadata & Badges (JetBrains Mono):** Injects authentic engineering DNA into XP counters, algorithmic snips, progress timers, and profile diagnostics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column dynamic grid designed for both touch tablets (kiosks) and student mobile screens:
- **Desktop / Kiosk Standee (1024px+):** 12 columns, 40px margins (`margin`), 24px gutters (`gutter`). Max container width is locked at 1200px for comfortable touch ergonomics without excessive arm travel.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (640px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters (`gutter-sm`).
- **Mobile Handheld (<640px):** 4 columns, 20px margins (`margin-mobile`), 12px gutters.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px grid system. Padding scale within gamification panels defaults to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop/tablet cards and collapses to `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports. Interactive hit targets are strictly scaled to a minimum of 48px to accommodate hasty physical touch interactions at high-traffic fair booths.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial hierarchy abandons standard directional drop shadows in favor of **Luminescent Glassmorphism** and **Chroma-Reflective HUD Tiers**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Dark obsidian gradient `#0B0F19` anchored with ambient radial glow patches using primary cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`) and neon violet (`rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`).
- **HUD Glass Tier 1 (Passive Containers):** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.65)`, backdrop blur `16px`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. No outer shadow.
- **HUD Glass Tier 2 (Interactive Modules):** Background `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.70)`, backdrop blur `20px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Outer aura: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Active State Glow Tier (Focus & Selected):** Card border updates to full archetype accent (e.g., `#06B6D4` or `#EC4899`). Layered ambient glow: `0 0 0 1px {accent_color}`, accompanied by `0 0 24px -2px {accent_color_alpha_35}`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances futuristic smoothness with tactile warmth:
- Standard containers, interactive question blocks, and game panels feature `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem / 24px) corners.
- Interactive buttons and inputs leverage generous rounded curves (`rounded-xl` to full pills) to ensure immediate clickability and arcade-like ergonomics.
- Monospace tags, XP chips, and profile identifiers maintain smaller rounded corners (`rounded-md` / 6px) to echo digital memory chips and HUD status readouts.

## Components

### Buttons & Arcade Action Triggers
- **Primary Cyber Button:** Height 56px minimum. Pill or `rounded-xl`. Solid gradient fill using electric blue to cyber cyan (`linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB, #06B6D4)`). Text set in `Outfit` Semibold 16px, dark or pure white with crisp contrast. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`. Ambient halo: `0 0 20px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`. Hover/Press initiates scale `0.98` with amplified bloom `0 0 28px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.7)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Height 56px. Translucent glass `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. On hover, border illuminates with the selected archetype's neon hue.

### Interactive Challenge Cards (Quiz & Logic Prompts)
- Constructed with Tier 1 Glassmorphism. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Option cards contain an embedded index pill (`JetBrains Mono`, e.g., `01`, `02`) that turns vibrant upon selection.
- Active/Selected state: Background shifts to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`, border illuminates to `#06B6D4`, casting a soft inner light (`inset 0 0 12px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)`).

### Profile Identity Badges & Chips
- Monospace badges (`JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase) encapsulating skill tags, e.g., `[SYS.AI_EXPLORER]`, `[NET.SECURITY]`.
- Styled with a saturated micro-border (`1px solid {profile_color}`) and 10% opacity colored background.

### Input Fields & Terminal Console Prompts
- Background: `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.8)`. Padding: `16px 20px`. Border: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Focus state: Border sparks to electric cyan (`#06B6D4`) with `0 0 16px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`. Monospace placeholder text (`JetBrains Mono` 14px) simulating an active IDE cursor.

### Gamified Metric Displays (XP Gauges & Radar Match)
- Thin track lines (`4px` or `8px` height) with dark slate backing (`#1F2937`) and neon gradient fills equipped with a pulsing terminal cap.
- Profile Result Module: Displays the finalized archetype card wrapped in a high-intensity dual-gradient glowing border, paired with dynamic technical stats and direct links to corresponding engineering degree tracks.
